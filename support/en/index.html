Support

Answers to the questions we hear most. If none of these solve it, please email us at the address below.

Contact: support@walkus.jp
SHIRUS is built by one person, so a reply may take a few days.

Recording doesn't start, or stops partway

Check that location access is set to Always (iPhone Settings → SHIRUS → Location). With "While Using the App", movement is not recorded while the app is closed.

Low Power Mode, or Background App Refresh turned off, can also make the recording coarser.

I purchased, but the rest of the map isn't showing

Go to Settings → "Purchase and unlock code…" → "Restore purchase". As long as you use the same Apple Account, you do not need to buy it again on a new device.

If this device already knows about your purchase, "Restore purchase" appears directly in Settings.

I want to use my records in another app

Settings → "Export records (GPX)…" writes your routes out as GPX. They open in Google Earth, QGIS, Strava and anything else that reads GPX. You can export everything as one file, or one file per record (zip).

Moving to a new device

Settings → "Back up records (file)…" saves a file, and "Restore from a backup…" loads it on the new device. Records live only on your device, so nothing carries over unless you do this.

There are places I don't want to show

There are three levels. Pick the one that matches what you want.

  • Hide — kept out of the map only. The data stays, and you can bring it back at any time.
  • Exclude from recording — nothing is recorded in that area from now on. ⚠ Turning it off does not bring back what was never recorded.
  • Delete — removes the records themselves. This cannot be undone.

⚠ Neither hiding nor deleting affects backup files you already exported, or images you already shared or saved.

I want to erase everything

Settings → "Erase all records…". Deleting the app erases them too. Neither can be undone.

Trains and ferries aren't detected correctly

Automatic train and ferry detection works in Japan only, because that is where the app carries the railway and ferry route data. Elsewhere, those trips are recorded as walking, cycling or driving.

Requirements

iPhone only. iPad is not supported. See the App Store listing for the supported iOS versions.
