Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

SHIRUS records the roads you walk. The records stay on your device. There is no account and no sign-in.

What is recorded

Location (latitude, longitude, accuracy, time), the tracks and painted roads built from it, and step counts. All of it is stored in a database on your device and never sent to a SHIRUS server (there is no server).

What leaves your device

  1. The map image: the area currently on screen is requested from the map tile provider (OpenFreeMap). That request includes a rough location. No name or ID is attached.
  2. App updates: the app checks for updates on launch (Expo update server). No location is sent.
  3. Purchases: handled by Apple. SHIRUS never receives your card details.

Device backups

Because the records live in a database on your device, they are included in your device's backups (iCloud backup, backups to a computer, and so on). How those backups are encrypted and where they are stored follows your device's and Apple's settings.

About sharing

You can share the map as an image, but only when you press the share button. Nothing leaves automatically.

Places left out of recording

Paint an area as "left out of recording" and no location is saved inside it. A "hidden area" keeps the records but does not draw them on the map (nor in shared images).

Diagnostics

Records kept on the device to work out later why something did not run (permission states, timestamps of processing). Nothing is sent automatically.

Ads and analytics

No ads. No behavioural analytics. No third-party tracking.

Deleting

Settings → "Erase all records" erases the records on your device. Deleting the app erases them too. Neither can be undone.

Map data

Map data is based on data by the OpenStreetMap contributors. © OpenStreetMap contributors

Changes to this policy

When the contents change, this page is updated and the "last updated" date at the top is revised.
